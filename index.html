Recent Publication

Nonlocal Elastic Unloading as a Mechanism for Breakdown Work Scaling in Laboratory Earthquakes

Ke, Chang, McLaskey, Kammer & Marone (2026) · Earth and Planetary Science Letters · doi: 10.1016/j.epsl.2026.120283

Breakdown work in our laboratory earthquakes comes mainly from nonlocal elastic unloading of the loading system rather than from dissipation near the rupture front. I performed all finite-element simulations and led the cohesive-zone-model fits.
