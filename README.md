## About

I work at the boundary of **seismology** and **structural engineering**, connecting laboratory
rupture physics, low-cost monitoring systems, and computational methods. My thesis is
*Secondary Weakening in dm-Scale Laboratory Earthquakes*.

- 🎓 B.S. in Physics and B.S. in Civil Engineering, NTU (2020–2025); M.S. in Structural Engineering, NTU (2025–2026)
- 🔬 Institute of Earth Sciences, Academia Sinica — seismic structural health monitoring and AI-based signal classification (2023–present)
- 🧪 Division of Structural Engineering, NTU — scaled laboratory earthquake experiments and LEFM/FEM modeling (2024–present)
- 🛠️ Python · C++ · Fortran · LaTeX

## Research directions

| # | Area | Focus |
|---|------|-------|
| 01 | **Laboratory Earthquakes** | Rupture processes, stress drop, and energy scaling via scaled experiments, passive acoustics, and LEFM-based interpretation |
| 02 | **Structural Health Monitoring** | QSIS-based monitoring workflows for buildings, vibration response, and structural safety |
| 03 | **Scientific Computing** | Pipelines in Python, C++, and Fortran for seismic analysis, simulation, machine learning, and reproducible research |
