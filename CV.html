Research Experience

Seismic Structural Health Monitoring and Analysis

2023-Present

Institute of Earth Sciences, Academia Sinica. Advisors: Dr. Kuo-Fong Ma, Dr. Wen-Tzong Liang

  • Contributed to QSIS, a low-cost MEMS seismic network in more than ten buildings in Taiwan, tracking building health with eigenfrequency, spectral, and deconvolution analyses.
  • Identified a coherent 3.906 Hz instrument artifact below the noise floor by stacking cross-correlation functions; traced it to the data loggers' cooling fans, prompting a network-wide switch to passive cooling.
  • Maintained seismic stations with IES technicians after the 2024 Mw 7.4 Hualien earthquake, co-locating QSIS sensors with observatory-grade seismometers.

Validation of Seismic Parameters via Laboratory Experiments

2024-Present

Division of Structural Engineering, Department of Civil Engineering, NTU. Advisor: Dr. Chun-Yu Ke

  • Performed all finite-element simulations (ABAQUS) of a double-direct-shear apparatus, showing that the acrylic loading block stores more than 10× the strain energy of the granite blocks and dominates the energy release [EPSL, 2026].
  • Led the cohesive-zone-model fits to near-fault strain records, showing near-front dissipation (< 10 J/m2) more than an order of magnitude below the breakdown work.
  • Designed and ran 1-D (50 cm) PMMA laboratory-earthquake experiments with strain-rosette arrays, and collaborated on 2-D (50 × 50 cm) experiments with piezoelectric (PZT) sensors; after an event at 28 MPa welded the fault, developed an optical laser-sheet method to detect frictional melting directly.
  • Implemented a 3-D dynamic rupture model with rate-and-state friction in tatva (JAX, differentiable finite elements), verified on SCEC/USGS TPV101/102 against UGUCA and SPECFEM3D (~1% RMS in slip).
