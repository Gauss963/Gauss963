JpGU 25 & 26 and some Graduation Frames

Posted on 2026-07-02 · Keywords: JpGU, Graduation, Film Photography, Travel Notes

These are scanned frames from my 2025 and 2026 JpGU trips in Japan, mixed with a few graduation photographs and the smaller moments that usually stay in memory longer than the official schedule.

I kept the sequence close to the scan order instead of forcing a strict itinerary. Some frames are bright, some are underexposed, and some are just quick notes taken in passing, but together they feel closer to how those two years actually looked from inside the trip.
